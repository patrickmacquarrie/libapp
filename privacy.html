Privacy

Last updated: July 31, 2026.

Through the Wall stores the information needed to run prediction pools: your sign-in identifier, email supplied by your authentication provider, username, pool memberships, predictions, progress, and optional Cast Chemistry ratings.

Who can see what

Poolmates can see your username, public progress, and eligible scores. Predictions are hidden until the applicable lock conditions are met. Friend-pool Chemistry scorecards are shared only when you opt in. Global Chemistry shows combined averages, not named individual scorecards.

Service providers

Firebase provides authentication, database, and server functions. GitHub Pages hosts the website. These providers process technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Retention and control

You can leave pools from Settings. You can also delete your account from Settings; this removes your profile and game data and deletes pools you own. Limited security and operational records may be retained where reasonably necessary.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.
